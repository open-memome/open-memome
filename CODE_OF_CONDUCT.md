# Code of Conduct

Open Memome discusses beliefs, identities, political systems, religions, health practices, and contested history. Precision and respect are required.

## Expected

- Address claims, sources, and scope directly.
- Describe communities in neutral language.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Correct errors openly.
- Make room for relevant cultural and disciplinary perspectives.

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or dehumanizing language
- Doxxing or publication of private information
- Coordinated manipulation of reviews
- Knowingly fabricated evidence or concealed conflicts
- Advocacy disguised as neutral documentation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these rules and may restrict repeat offenders. Report abusive GitHub content through GitHub's reporting tools and flag the relevant issue or pull request for maintainer attention.
