# Governance

Open Memome is maintained in public. Its authority comes from inspectable evidence, review, and version history.

## Roles

**Contributors** propose records, evidence, reviews, code, or corrections.

**Reviewers** evaluate a specific claim and explain their reasoning. Reviewers do not need repository write access.

**Maintainers** triage contributions, enforce scope, merge accepted changes, and publish releases. Maintainers must disclose material conflicts of interest and should not be the sole reviewer of their own disputed contribution.

## Decisions

Routine corrections require one maintainer review and passing automated checks. New documented records require evidence of recurrence and maintainer review; disputed records require an additional independent reviewer. Changes to definitions, status rules, metrics, licences, or governance require a public proposal and a decision record.

No contributor has permanent authority over a topic. Expertise informs review but does not replace evidence.

## Disputes

Open a dispute issue against one record, source, relationship, or decision. State the contested claim, provide evidence, and propose a resolution. Maintainers may mark a record `contested` while review continues.

Outcomes and credible minority rationales remain linked from the record. Reversals are new commits; history is not rewritten to hide earlier decisions.

## Maintainer succession

New maintainers should have a sustained record of careful contributions and constructive reviews. At least two organization owners should eventually be maintained for continuity. Access is removed after prolonged inactivity, compromised credentials, serious misconduct, or misuse of project authority.

## Releases

The default branch is releasable. Automated checks must pass before merge. Releases summarize changes to the corpus, method, and software. Data snapshots receive a version and date.
