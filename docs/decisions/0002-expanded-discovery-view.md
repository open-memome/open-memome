# 0002: Show a balanced discovery sample by default

Date: 2026-08-22

Status: accepted

## Decision

Show scoped records alongside a balanced sample of high-reach discovery leads in the default map. Select up to 55 eligible leads per domain, then add internet-meme records above the published reach threshold. Keep every discovery lead hollow and labelled as unassessed.

## Why

The scoped corpus is too sparse to reveal the shape of the wider memome. Showing the full discovery backlog would overwhelm the canvas and imply that every catalogue match had passed review. A balanced sample exposes useful coverage while preserving the distinction between a lead and an assessed meme.

## Consequences

The expanded layer is a review queue, not a verified corpus. Wikimedia sitelinks bias the sample toward encyclopedic coverage and established topics. Contributors can filter to scoped or documented records, and the selection rule remains public and versioned.
