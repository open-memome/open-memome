# Roadmap

## Current

- Public contribution and review workflow
- Canonical IDs, aliases, and duplicate resolution
- Honest separation of documented records, candidates, and discovery leads
- Reliable search, zoom, filtering, and reach display
- Assessment of high-impact discovery leads across all domains

## Next

- Record-level pages with stable URLs and decision history
- Comparable reach and attention observations with dates and sources
- Community Notes-style cross-perspective validation based on review history, not raw vote totals
- Relationship overlays for variants, influence, opposition, and reinforcement
- Versioned data releases and documented coverage audits

## Later, after evidence supports it

- Historical influence slider
- Language- and region-specific map views
- Community and organization memome comparisons with appropriate privacy controls

Items move forward only when the required data and review process exist. The roadmap is not a promise of dates.
